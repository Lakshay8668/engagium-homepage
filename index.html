Frequently asked questions

What does Engagium do?

Engagium provides businesses with digital tools they can use to automate WhatsApp conversations, collect and manage Google reviews, share digital menus and NFC cards, manage leads in a CRM and launch a website with AI — all from one platform.

Is Engagium built on the official WhatsApp Business API?

Yes. Our WhatsApp solutions run on the official WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta, in line with WhatsApp’s policies, so your business messaging stays compliant.

Do I need a technical team to use Engagium?

No. Our tools are designed to be simple and easy to start with, with no complicated setup or technical expertise needed. Our team also helps with onboarding.
